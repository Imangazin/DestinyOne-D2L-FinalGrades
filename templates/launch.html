PCS Final Grade Transfer (Brightspace to Modern Campus)

Professional and Continuing Studies instructors can use this tool to transfer Brightspace Final Calculated Grades to the Modern Campus system for final approval.

The options below will pull the Final Calculated Grade column from this site's Grades page and validate it against the grading requirements. A Final Letter Grade will be assigned based on the following grade scheme:

{% for grade_range in grade_scheme %}
{{ grade_range.letter }} {{ grade_range.display_range }}
{% endfor %}

Manual adjustments or status changes, such as Incomplete, must be performed in Modern Campus. Please communicate any concerns to PCS staff.

Grades of A, B, or C are considered passing if the course is Pass/Fail.

Select the Course Section to be transferred and then click Validate. Review the list of students, their grades, and check for any warnings. Click Transfer Grades to submit for final approval.
